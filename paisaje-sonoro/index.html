Paisaje Sonoro

Performance audiovisual en vivo que utiliza una instalación interactiva site-specific como interfaz ← Back to Artist Work





Paisaje Sonoro is a live audiovisual performance that uses a site-specific interactive installation as its interface. The work unfolds through live activation, where sound and visuals emerge in real time through the body and its interaction with a responsive physical environment.

The performance employs a biophilic structure composed of elemental materials such as light, water, air, earth, and electricity. Rather than functioning as an autonomous installation, this structure operates as an interface through which audiovisual processes are triggered and shaped during the performance.

Over the course of approximately thirty minutes, the performer activates the system through bodily presence, movement, and stillness. Plant biodata emitted by living organisms temporarily inhabiting the installation is translated into sound and visual processes, generating an evolving audiovisual landscape.

Paisaje Sonoro unfolds as a durational and perceptual experience rather than a fixed composition. Through attention, listening, and embodied presence, the performance explores how bodies, technological systems, and living environments co-exist and influence one another in real time.
